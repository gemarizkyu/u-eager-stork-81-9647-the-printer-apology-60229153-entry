Entry for the **"The Printer Apology"** bounty on make.fun.

One line, 108 characters, as an apology note from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it: the printer choked on a staple, panicked, and ate the only copy — then lands the punch on the office's own habit of never backing anything up. Apology frame, real printer grievances, punch at the very end.

The entry text:

> Sorry about the jam. I choked on a staple, panicked, and ate your only copy. We both know you never back up.
