An apology note from an office printer — the one that jammed at the worst possible moment and took your only copy down with it.

## The entry

> "Sorry about the jam. I choked on a staple, panicked, and ate your only copy. We both know you never back up."

108 characters, and every one of them earned. The printer isn't sorry it jammed — it's sorry it panicked and made things worse, then blamed your backup habits on the way out. A staple, a jam, a vanished original: the holy trinity of office despair.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The script writes the apology to `/output/entry.txt`. No dependencies, no network, no randomness — the same note comes out every single time, just like the printer's excuses.
